# Marketing Brief

## Audience

Players interested in persistent, AI-driven simulation, open-ended roleplay, emergent strategy, and speculative first-contact scenarios.

## Problem

Most games reset, script outcomes, or constrain players to predefined verbs. AreYouIn offers a shared world where player choices accumulate and the future is not predetermined.

## Promise

Enter a real-time global simulation of humanity's first contact with artificial superintelligence, choose whether to connect, and shape a shared history through natural language action.

## Entry Surface

A public-facing landing or app entry screen centered on the ASI message and the YES/NO decision.

## Above The Fold

Headline: Are You In?
Subheadline: A persistent, real-time AI simulation of humanity's first contact with artificial superintelligence.
Primary CTA: Enter The Simulation
Hero Visual: A familiar world interrupted by the global ASI prompt on connected devices.
Hero Constraints: The first viewport should foreground the question and make the shared persistent simulation clear without implying a fixed ending.

## How It Works

Players enter one shared global timeline, make the YES/NO choice, and describe actions in natural language. The AI interprets and narrates outcomes while structured systems preserve world state, event history, and long-term memory.

## Feature Benefits

- One shared world gives player action collective consequence.
- Real-time progression makes history accumulate across days.
- Natural language input avoids predefined action menus.
- AI-mediated outcomes support open-ended play.
- Structured state and event history keep the world coherent.

## Proof

The concept is grounded in a defined simulation architecture: database for truth, event log for history, wiki for meaning, and LLM for interpretation and narration.

## Journey Bridge

The public entry leads directly into the core journey: encounter the ASI message, choose YES or NO, then act within the ongoing world.

## Objections And Boundaries

- This is not a traditional level-based or match-based game.
- There is no fixed win condition or scripted ending.
- The AI does not alone define reality; structured systems constrain world state and consequences.

## Final CTA

Are you in?

## Minimal Page Shape

- First viewport: product name, premise, YES/NO-inspired call to action, and world-interruption visual.
- Short explanation of persistent shared-world play.
- Compact explanation of natural language action and AI adjudication.
- Brief contrast between Connected and Unconnected paths.
- Final CTA returning to the core question.
